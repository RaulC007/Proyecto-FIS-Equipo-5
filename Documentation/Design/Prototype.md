## Prototype Verification

- FR1 School Control procedure catalog: ✅ Met. Selecting a certificate option shows the name, requirement (be enrolled), cost ($50 MXN), 3 to 5 business days, and mode (online form and in-person pickup). The use cases already treat the chat options as the way to select a procedure.
- FR2 Conversational chat: ✅ Met. The prototype has a chat window, a text input, and replies with numbered steps and bullet points.
- FR4 Certificates and special format: ✅ Met. The cost, payment method, and instructions for requesting a special format are shown.
- FR5 Service, contacts and external referral: ✅ Met. Service hours, location, institutional email, and phone are reachable in 2 clicks or fewer, and referral to external departments is shown.
- FR6 Links and templates components: ✅ Met. The official form button appears in the certificate replies. The requirement asks for forms or templates, so the form link satisfies it.
- NFR1 Structure and readability: ✅ Met. The prototype uses a clear typographic hierarchy, short text blocks, bullet points, and numbered lists, with paragraphs of 4 lines or fewer.
- NFR4 Ease of navigation and interface: ✅ Met. Every topic is reached in 3 clicks or fewer (open the assistant, select an option), and it works the same on.
