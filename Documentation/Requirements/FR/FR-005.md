# Functional-Requirement-005
>Priority and Justification: Medium, It guides students to the correct office when their query falls outside School Control scope.

## Requirement Name
Service, Contacts and External Referral.

## Requirement Description
The system must display service window hours, physical location of School Control, institutional email, phone, and contact cards for external departments.

## Acceptance Criteria
Given a user looking for operational or external contact information, when navigating to the service panel, then the system presents validated contact cards and office hours accessible within 5 seconds and without confusing or mixing any of the panels under normal conditions.
