# Functional-Requirement-001
>Priority and Justification: High, It is the main navigation feature that directly resolves the most common student inquiries. 

## Requirement Name
Catalog of School Control Procedures.

## Requirement Description
The system must provide detailed information for each available academic procedure, including its name, mandatory preliminary requirements, current cost, delivery time in business days, and whether it is in-person or online when requested by the user.

## Acceptance Criteria
 Given a user navigating the procedure catalog, when they click or select a procedure card, then the system displays all 5 mandatory metadata fields (name, requirements, cost, business days, and mode) in 5.0 second or less with 100% data accuracy under normal conditions.
