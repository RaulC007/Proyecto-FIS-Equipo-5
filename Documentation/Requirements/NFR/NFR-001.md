# Non-Functional-Requirement-001
>Priority and Justification: High, It ensures that procedure information is easy to scan and understand, which is essential for new students who are unfamiliar with school processes.

## Requirement Name
Usability - Structure and Readability.

## Requirement Description
The system must organize all interface layouts, procedure instructions, and automated responses using short text blocks (no more than 250 words), bullet points, or numbered lists (with a maximum limit of 40 words per point) and clear typographical hierarchy to ensure information is easy to scan, read completely, and actionable. 

## Acceptance Criteria
Given a user viewing procedure instructions or requesting multi-step guidance via the chat interface, when content or automated responses are rendered on screen, then the system presents paragraphs of no more than 250 words and utilizes bullet points or numbered lists without exceeding 40 words per point, maintaining a clear typographical hierarchy.
