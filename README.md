# FMAT UADY "Control Escolar" Vistual Assistant

>Project Status: Phase 1, Requirements

## Objective 
The objective of our project is to create a chatbot that allows users to enter prompts in natural language that help them make information queries and resolve confusions they may have when they need to interact with the School Control office of the Faculty of Mathematics; and for the chatbot to be capable of providing the requested information, giving the means to carry out the processes in case the student meets the requirements, or in cases where the information request is very specific, providing the methods to contact the School Control office to solve their problem; all of this in the same natural language.

## Project Scope 
The scope of the project is to be able to resolve the doubts and/or needs that students of the Faculty of Mathematics may have regarding the School Control office, offering truthful, clear, and concise information about what they need, the limitations would arise at the moment of having to explain that some processes do not correspond to the School Control office but rather to other instances, since we would not have much information given that it is not the instance that corresponds to us, cases that require special attention from School Control staff such as certificates with specific data, and to a lesser extent the speed of updating the information the chatbot has.

## Users
The project is currently oriented toward new-entry students, with the most common doubts that may arise, however, any student of the Faculty of Mathematics could use the application.
- Primary users: New-entry students in the Faculty of Mathematics (Students who need certificates and do not know the information or the process required to process them).
- Secondary users: Students who are not new entries (Students preparing for the graduation process).
- Potential users: Applicants to any Bachelor's degree offered in the Faculty of Mathematics (Information regarding the admission processes to a Bachelor's degree).

## Value proposition
New-entry students usually arrive at the faculty with many doubts about procedures, paperwork, and dates. Today the only way to resolve them is to go directly to School Control, which involves limitations of schedule, availability, and saturation, since it is not possible to attend to all students at the same time. As a consequence, the staff ends up answering the same questions over and over again, and students do not always receive a timely response.
Our product is a chatbot that responds in natural language, at any time and from a single point of access, to the most frequent doubts about School Control procedures. Unlike going to the service window, it does not depend on service hours nor generate lines, and it delivers information quickly, clearly, and concisely. When the case is special or does not correspond to School Control, the chatbot guides the student toward the responsible person or the appropriate department, instead of leaving them without an answer.
The proposal is valuable because it offers students more accessible attention and reduces their uncertainty from the first contact with the procedures. At the same time, it allows School Control staff to stop attending to repetitive queries and dedicate their time to situations that truly require their intervention.
